Questions

Good to know

Does my child need to be an AMA member?

No. Camp is open to everyone aged 5 to 13, beginners included. New to AMA? Book a free trial class before camp so your child meets the instructors and walks in on day one feeling confident. Tick the box when you book and we'll call you to choose a time.

What should they wear and bring?

Their martial arts uniform or comfortable clothes. Pack lunch, snacks and a water bottle (no nuts). Fridays are superhero outfit day, so they can come dressed as their favourite superhero.

Who runs camp?

AMA black belt instructors who are child safety cleared and first aid qualified, led by our camp coordinator Hakan.

How many instructors are there?

At least one instructor for every 8 children, every day.

How much does it cost?

$80 a day, or $70 a day when you book 5 or more days for a child. Payment is taken when you book.

Can I change days or get a refund?

Yes, if you let us know at least 48 hours before the camp day. We'll move your child to another day or refund that day. Call (02) 9569 1943.

Can I stay after 3pm?

Camp finishes with the parent show at 3pm. Members can stay for their afternoon class: add it to the booking form and we'll look after them.
