Questions

Good to know

Does my child need to be an AMA member?

No. Parents' Night Out is open to all kids aged 3 to 12. It's a great way for friends to try AMA.

Is dinner included?

Yes. Dinner and snacks are included. Tell us about any allergies when you book, and please don't send food containing nuts.

What should they wear and bring?

Comfortable clothes they can move in, and a water bottle. Christmas outfits and Santa hats are very welcome!

Who looks after the kids?

Our AMA instructors run the whole night, with every child signed in and out.

How much does it cost?

$60 per child, paid when you book.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Let us know at least 48 hours before the night for a full refund. Call (02) 9569 1943.
